Qinyue Fei

Astronomy & Astrophysics, University of Toronto

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Toronto (since July 2025), working with Prof. Seiji Fujimoto. I received my PhD from the Kavli Institute for Astronomy and Astrophysics (KIAA) at Peking University in 2025, under the supervision of Prof. Ran Wang. During 2023 to 2024, I was a visiting student at the Kavli IPMU, University of Tokyo, under Prof. John D. Silverman.

I am interested in the physics of active galactic nuclei (AGNs) and how supermassive black holes co-evolve with their host galaxies across cosmic time — from molecular gas dynamics and quasar feedback in local AGNs, to the host galaxies of z ≳ 6 quasars, to little red dots and intermediate-mass black holes discovered with JWST. Feel free to check out my projects for more details on my work.

Beyond research, I enjoy playing badminton and going hiking.
